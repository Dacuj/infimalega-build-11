AMORE TOSSICO

2026

Amore Tossico examines proximity without lineage.

Off-white cotton, intentionally stained and structurally stressed, defines the surface language of the capsule. Irregular puffed sleeves alter proportion while arms remain consistently covered, limiting exposure and gesture.

The garments operate as uniform fragments assembled into a non-biological family.

Each piece exists as a singular 1/1 archive unit.
