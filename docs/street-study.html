/Street Study

Street Study. Romantic punk in the urban land.

It refers in particular to a series of colors, clothes and shapes typical of the 80s, with makeup referring to some cult films of those years. Specifically to romantic punk and new wave, all of which are revisited by Infimalega.

Each piece exists as a singular 1/1 archive unit.
