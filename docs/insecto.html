insecto

2025

Insecto stages a small circus formation in progressive decay.

Vertical red striping establishes a regimental rhythm. Polka dot surfaces and exaggerated volumes reference ceremonial costume without preserving its function. Cosmetic intensity disrupts theatrical balance.

The capsule studies spectacle as structural corrosion.

Each garment is produced as an individual archive piece, fixed between display and deterioration.
